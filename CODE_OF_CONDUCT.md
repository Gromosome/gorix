# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Gorix is an open source project. Contributors, maintainers, sponsors, and users are expected to participate respectfully and professionally.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and receiving technical feedback constructively.
- Keeping discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them when needed.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning language.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Abusive, discriminatory, or threatening conduct.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, community channels, and project-related events. It also applies when someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

**pressnavek@gromosome.com**

Reports should include relevant context, links, screenshots, usernames, dates, and any requested privacy constraints. Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take actions appropriate to the situation, including:

- Clarifying expectations or issuing a warning.
- Editing, hiding, or removing comments or content.
- Temporarily or permanently limiting participation.
- Escalating serious threats or illegal behavior to appropriate authorities.

Enforcement decisions should prioritize the safety of the community and the integrity of the project.
